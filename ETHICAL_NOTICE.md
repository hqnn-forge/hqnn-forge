# ETHICAL NOTICE

*Last updated: 2026-10-22*

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document expresses the ethical intent and community values of the hqnn-forge project.

**Important: This document is not a software licence.** The sole licence governing this software is the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Nothing in this document alters or restricts the rights granted under that licence.

hqnn-forge is an open-source research library for hybrid quantum-classical machine learning. It combines PennyLane quantum circuits with PyTorch models and focuses on evaluating quantum layers against classical controls with matched parameter counts, particularly for imbalanced binary classification on tabular data.

The project is intended to support academic, educational, and civilian research. Its open availability reflects the project's commitment to reproducible experimentation, scientific transparency, and responsible collaboration.

## 2. Uses Consistent with the Project's Intent

The following activities are consistent with the project's intent:

* Academic and scientific research into hybrid quantum-classical machine learning.
* Educational instruction, student projects, and learning about quantum machine learning.
* Civilian research and development using quantum and classical machine-learning techniques.
* Reproducible benchmarking, model evaluation, and comparison of quantum and classical approaches.
* Open scientific collaboration, including the responsible sharing of experimental methods and results.

Use, modification, and distribution of the software remain governed by the Apache License 2.0.

## 3. Uses Inconsistent with the Project's Intent

The following uses are inconsistent with the project's ethical intent:

* Military, defense, or weapons-related development.
* Surveillance, intelligence gathering, or systems intended to facilitate the targeting or monitoring of individuals without appropriate safeguards.
* Dual-use applications that support military or surveillance objectives.
* Applications that facilitate violations of human rights or disregard applicable legal and ethical responsibilities.

The maintainers ask users and contributors to refrain from engaging in, supporting, or facilitating these activities through the use or development of hqnn-forge.

This section expresses the project's ethical expectations. It does not establish additional software-licensing restrictions or modify the permissions granted by the Apache License 2.0.

## 4. Openness and Research Context

hqnn-forge is publicly available as an open-source research project. Its source code and documentation are published to support inspection, reproducibility, and collaboration.

The project's stated research focus includes:

* Hybrid quantum-classical classifiers implemented using PennyLane and PyTorch.
* Evaluation of quantum models against classical controls with matched parameter counts.
* Experiments involving imbalanced binary tabular classification.
* Model evaluation, statistical comparisons, ablation studies, and circuit diagnostics.

The project investigates whether a quantum layer provides a measurable benefit relative to its parameter cost. Its existence does not establish that quantum models are universally superior to classical models or that quantum advantage is guaranteed.

The software is provided for research and educational purposes. Its public availability and stated research focus should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every possible application is appropriate, lawful, or ethically acceptable.

Users and contributors are responsible for understanding and complying with the laws and regulations applicable to their activities, including any relevant export-control, sanctions, privacy, and data-protection requirements.

## 5. Contributor Principles

By contributing to hqnn-forge, contributors are expected to respect the project's ethical intent and community principles.

Contributors should:

* Have the right to submit the code, documentation, or other material they contribute.
* Respect applicable laws, third-party rights, and relevant research and data-protection requirements.
* Report experimental results accurately and avoid misleading claims about model performance or quantum advantage.
* Support reproducibility, transparency, and responsible scientific collaboration.
* Consider the potential consequences of their work and refrain from knowingly supporting the uses identified as inconsistent with the project's intent.
* Follow the repository's contribution guidelines and Code of Conduct.

Contributors remain responsible for the legality and appropriateness of their own contributions and activities.
